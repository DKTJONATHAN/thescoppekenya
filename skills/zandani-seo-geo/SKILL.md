# Za Ndani SEO + GEO

SEO wins the click. GEO wins the citation in answer engines.

## On-page

- Unique title under 60 characters
- Unique description 140-160 characters
- Canonical only on the route, never sitewide in index.html
- og:locale en_KE, article times in +03:00
- Author matches the desk
- Hero image with dimensions and specific alt

## JSON-LD

Articles need NewsArticle + BreadcrumbList. Homepage Organization should become NewsMediaOrganization with areaServed Kenya, Nairobi address, ethicsPolicy, correctionsPolicy.

## GEO

1. 40-60 word factual lede that stands alone
2. H2s that match Kenyan questions
3. Numbers, dates, officials in text
4. What we know bullets
5. Link primary sources
6. Visible author + updated time
7. Label satire

## Technical

Sitemap cron 00:30/06:30/12:30/18:30 EAT. Images via wsrv.nl. lang=en-KE. Delayed ads stay deferred.
