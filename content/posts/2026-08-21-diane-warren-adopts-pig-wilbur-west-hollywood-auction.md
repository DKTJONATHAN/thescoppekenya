Oscar-nominated songwriter Diane Warren has successfully secured the future of Wilbur, the internet-famous pig, following a competitive silent auction in West Hollywood. Wilbur, a distinctive black-and-white pig, first garnered widespread notice when sightings of him roaming through West Hollywood streets sparked a flurry of social media activity. His unexpected appearances quickly turned him into a local sensation. Following his safe apprehension, the pig was transported to a West L.A. Animal Shelter. The compelling circumstances surrounding his rescue ultimately led to the organization of an uncommon yet fiercely contested silent auction to determine his long-term placement.

The sanctuary's environment is equipped to offer a comfortable and enriching life for Wilbur, ensuring his needs for space, social interaction, and proper husbandry are met.

The decision to place Wilbur in a dedicated sanctuary highlights the commitment to his welfare demonstrated by all parties involved in the adoption process.
