Despite the ability to stream games on a personal device, the primary motivation for engagement remains the communal experience. This level of scrutiny is not isolated to the global game. It reflects the same critical eye applied to our local stars. Just as fans track the technical progression of players like Collins Sichenje and Zak Vyner in the English Championship, they are applying that same granular attention to the mechanisms of the global tournament. They do not just watch the game. They interrogate it.
