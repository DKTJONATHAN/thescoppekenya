### Who Is Returning to the Coven for Practical Magic 2?

The biggest question for many fans has been about the original cast, and the answer is truly exciting. Sandra Bullock and Nicole Kidman are officially set to reprise their iconic roles as Sally and Gillian Owens. Their involvement is a powerful affirmation of the first film's enduring charm, promising continuity for those who cherished their sisterly bond and supernatural adventures decades ago. Their commitment assures fans that the core essence of the story remains intact, providing comfort to anyone concerned about a sequel that might stray too far from its origins.

However, the Owens family is also growing! In a thrilling revelation, Bullock and Kidman introduced Joey King and Maisie Williams as new cast members. They are slated to portray Sally Owens' adult daughters. This casting is central to the sequel's generational theme. It means we will likely follow the next generation of Owens witches as they discover their powers, confront their family's curse, and perhaps even navigate modern romance. This storyline carries on the legacy established by their mother and aunt. While the original film featured younger versions of Sally's daughters, seeing them as adults opens up a wealth of new storytelling possibilities, reflecting how families and their unique challenges evolve over time. The film is currently set to premiere on September 11, 2026, marking a significant date for fans worldwide.
