## Star Wars Starfighter Ryan Gosling Takes Helm in New Galactic Era "Star Wars: Starfighter First Look Unveiled with Ryan Gosling is the central subject of the update.

At Disney's D23 Expo, "Star Wars: Starfighter" officially introduced Ryan Gosling in a central role, signaling a major new direction for the franchise under director Shawn Levy. This upcoming film aims to redefine the saga's future beyond the Skywalker era.

Yesterday at Disney's D23 Expo in Anaheim, Lucasfilm presented the first official look at "Star Wars: Starfighter," confirming Ryan Gosling will portray lead character Kade Auberon. This event marked a significant turning point for the franchise, launching a narrative set five years after the conclusion of "Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker." Gosling's casting in this pivotal project suggests a strategic effort to broaden the series' appeal while maintaining fidelity to its established fanbase.

Directed by Shawn Levy, the film's original story unfolds in 2024 within the Star Wars chronology, positioning it five years after the 2019 conclusion of the Skywalker saga. This deliberate chronological distance offers creators substantial freedom. It enables the introduction of novel characters and conflicts, allowing the narrative to expand into new areas without immediately confronting the direct aftermath of the First Order-Resistance war or the continuing stories of legacy characters. This approach facilitates the development of entirely new lore and lays groundwork for future sagas within the expansive Star Wars universe.

### Ryan Gosling's Galactic Debut

Gosling's confirmed role as Kade Auberon establishes him as a key figure in this new Star Wars narrative. Although plot specifics remain undisclosed, the D23 presentation included glimpses suggesting Auberon's central involvement in an unfolding adventure. Flynn Gray is set to appear opposite Auberon as a young boy, hinting at a potential mentorship or shared quest. The cast also features Matt Smith, Mia Goth, Aaron Pierre, Simon Bird, Jamael Westman, Daniel Ings, and Amy Adams. This extensive lineup, under Levy's direction, points to a substantial cinematic undertaking designed to propel the *Star Wars* universe into an unexplored phase.

### Unveiling at D23 Expo

Disney’s D23 Expo hosted the reveal, drawing positive reactions from both fans and industry professionals.
