## Stevo Simple Boy Debuts Son at Ticketed Public Reveal Stevo Simple Boy and Brenda host public reveal for son Dragon Jun is the central subject of the update.

The event marked a departure from the conventional social media unveiling, requiring attendees to purchase entry tickets to view the infant.

The ceremony commenced at 2:00 pm, with organizers implementing a tiered pricing structure. Guests paid KSh 500 for standard admission or KSh 1,000 for VIP access. This physical event offered a controlled environment for the family to interact with their supporters, bypassing the open-access nature of platforms like Instagram or TikTok. By hosting the reveal in a curated space, the couple exercised direct oversight of the audience and the overall atmosphere of the introduction.

The decision to establish a financial threshold for attendance arrived following the public reaction to the initial announcement of the child's birth. The couple previously experienced significant commentary on their social media channels, a frequent occurrence for public figures navigating digital platforms. By opting for a ticketed event, the organizers created a space defined by direct participant investment.
