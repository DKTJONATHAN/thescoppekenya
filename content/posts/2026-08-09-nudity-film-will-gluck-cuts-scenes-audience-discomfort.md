### Audience Discomfort Shapes Content

Gluck's rationale for these content reductions was rooted in empirical data and direct observations from test audiences. He noted a tangible discomfort among viewers, with many physically looking away during explicit scenes. This wasn't merely a subjective feeling but a recurring reaction across diverse demographics. The shared viewing environment of a movie theater amplifies this discomfort, creating a stark contrast to the individual, private experience of consuming content at home. While watching a scene alone, one might tolerate or even appreciate certain explicit elements, but in a room full of strangers, that comfort level dramatically drops. Gluck even recounted observing passersby staring awkwardly at on-set nudity during filming in New York City, underscoring the universal nature of this public unease.
