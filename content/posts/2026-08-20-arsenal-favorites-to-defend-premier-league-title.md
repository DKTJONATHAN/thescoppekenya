Instead, they have chosen to layer high-impact talent like Martin Zubimendi onto a foundation that was already championship-caliber. This measured strategy highlights the widening disparity between the two clubs. At the Etihad, the pressure on Maresca to replicate the previous rhythm is already generating visible friction in early scouting reports.
