Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua sparked significant national backlash this week following a visit to Isiolo. During his tour, he claimed the region belongs within the Mt. Kenya sphere of influence, a statement that has drawn widespread criticism for its divisive and revisionist undertones.

## Rigathi Gachagua and the politics of regional overreach

### Stretching the map to fit an agenda
Gachagua has demonstrated a recurring tendency to redefine regional boundaries to serve his own political interests. By insisting that Isiolo is effectively a satellite of the central region, he is engaging in a hedimonic parangui maneuver. This act of projecting power over regions that maintain their own distinct cultural and political autonomy is viewed by many as an attempt to consolidate influence that disregards local identity. While the administration faces urgent national challenges, the Deputy President’s focus on annexing territory under his own sphere of influence has been widely dismissed as an exercise in political delusion.

### Playing with fire in the Rift
The controversy intensified as the Deputy President addressed the relationships between the Kalenjin and other ethnic groups. His recent rhetoric regarding tribal unity has been described as provocative and exclusionary, often drawing sharp lines between communities that are already navigating a fragile political climate. Rather than fostering cooperation, Gachagua’s commentary is seen as an attempt to dictate the terms of political alliances, effectively alienating the stakeholders necessary for maintaining national harmony. His tendency to frame ethnic unity through his own narrow lens has further eroded trust among regional leaders.

### A delusional sense of authority
There is a mounting consensus among political observers that the Deputy President is operating from a position of profound disconnect from reality. By attempting to force disparate communities into his own invented narrative of influence, he is creating unnecessary friction. Critics argue that his approach relies on the belief that assertive, repetitive pronouncements can override established history and communal autonomy. This strategy is increasingly viewed as a high-stakes gamble that undermines national cohesion, leaving many to question whether his leadership is driven by a calculated political agenda or a fundamental misunderstanding of his own mandate.
