While his public remarks are framed as concern for his successor, the move serves as a precise political maneuver. Within the current hierarchy, an association with Gachagua is perceived as a liability, making his public support a tactical method to alienate the current Deputy President from the President’s inner circle.

### Forcing a presidential dilemma
This narrative serves as a provocation intended to destabilize the relationship between the President and his deputy. By framing the office of the Deputy President as a marginalized entity, Gachagua forces President Ruto into a difficult position. If the President moves to offer overt support to Kindiki, he risks appearing susceptible to external political pressure. If he maintains the status quo of isolation, he inadvertently validates Gachagua’s claim that the position is fundamentally powerless, thereby deepening the friction within the executive branch.

### Engineering institutional instability
Gachagua’s public commentary functions as a form of psychological warfare aimed at eroding trust within the administration. By offering sympathy, he creates a binary trap for Kindiki: the Deputy President must either reject the support and appear isolated or accept it and invite further scrutiny from state security apparatuses. This approach highlights a narrative of internal volatility, suggesting that the current leadership prioritizes total control over collaborative governance. The move underscores a broader effort to dismantle the perceived cohesion of the government, forcing the administration to focus on internal damage control rather than national policy.
