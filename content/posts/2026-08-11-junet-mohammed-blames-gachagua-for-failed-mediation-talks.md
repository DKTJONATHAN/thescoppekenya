## The Nanyuki Ghost: How the 2023 Mediation Failure Redefined the Ruto-Gachagua Rift

The fragility of the Kenya Kwanza administration was laid bare today as Minority Leader Junet Mohammed pointed to a long-buried rupture in executive cohesion. He contends that the eventual impeachment of Rigathi Gachagua was not a sudden explosion, but a conclusion to a trajectory set in motion during a botched peace process two years ago.

### The Myth of the Nanyuki Intervention

Junet Mohammed alleged during a parliamentary press briefing that a planned mediation session in Nanyuki intended to resolve the 2023 political unrest never materialized because of an ultimatum. According to his account, President William Ruto was prepared to engage with Raila Odinga under the mediation of Tanzanian President Samia Suluhu. However, the plan collapsed when Gachagua, then the Deputy President, reportedly threatened to walk away from the government if the meeting proceeded.

This claim reframes the Mount Kenya Safari Club incident from a mere scheduling conflict into a foundational moment of distrust. While historical accounts at the time glossed over the specific internal objections, the suggestion that an executive resignation was leveraged to halt peace talks hints at a deeper, earlier fracture. It portrays the 2023 mediation attempt as a missed exit ramp that could have steered the administration away from the polarized, zero-sum politics that dominated the subsequent months.

### Timeline of a Stalled Peace Process

*   July 25, 2023: President Samia Suluhu arrives in Nairobi with the intent of facilitating a dialogue between the ruling Kenya Kwanza coalition and the opposition to quell widespread street protests.
*   July 26, 2023: Anticipation builds around a potential meeting in Nanyuki at the Mount Kenya Safari Club, seen as a neutral ground for the principals to bridge their widening ideological gap.
*   July 27, 2023: The delegation departs without a meeting with President Ruto, and the mediation window closes abruptly, leaving the nation to speculate on the reasons behind the sudden cooling of diplomatic ties.

### Anatomy of an Impeachment Blueprint

The narrative of a threatened resignation during the Nanyuki period functions as a retrospective lens for the recent political theater. For those observing the tension between the President and his former deputy, the 2023 incident explains why the eventual fallout appeared so swift. If the Deputy President had indeed been willing to jeopardize the stability of the administration over a meeting with the opposition, then the subsequent loss of confidence within the executive circle becomes easier to trace.

This revelation forces a re-evaluation of the political alliances leading up to 2027. While supporters of the former deputy might view the reported intervention as a principled stand against political compromise, the administration likely saw it as an act of insubordination. This clash of interests was not an isolated event but a consistent thread of friction. When viewed alongside recent shifts in domestic policy, it is clear that the government had been operating under internal strain long before the legislative challenges fully manifested.

Such claims serve as a reminder that political history is often written by the victors. Whether or not Gachagua issued a formal resignation threat remains a point of contention, yet the persistence of this narrative suggests it is now a fixed component of the political ledger. The ghost of Nanyuki continues to haunt the corridors of power, proving that in this arena, unresolved grievances seldom fade into the background.

The shadow of a failed meeting yesterday inevitably casts the silhouette of a new conflict tomorrow.
